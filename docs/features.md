# Features

## Sidebar and tabs

- **Vertical sidebar** with the address, favorites, pinned tabs and today's
  tabs. Put it on the left or right, hide it with ⌘S, and in full screen it
  peeks out when you move to the edge.
- **Favorites**: big icon tiles at the top of the sidebar, shared by every
  space that uses the same profile.
- **Pinned tabs and folders** that stay put, with folders you can nest and
  collapse.
- **Today's tabs** below a divider. Clear them all at once, or let them archive
  themselves after a while (12 hours to 30 days, or never).
- **Archived tabs** under History → Archived Tabs, and searchable from the
  command bar.
- **Tab switcher**: ⌃Tab shows recent tabs as cards with snapshots.
- **Drag and drop** to reorder tabs, move them into folders, pin them or make
  them favorites.
- **Reopen closed tab** with ⇧⌘T.

## Spaces

- **Spaces** group tabs by context, such as Personal, Work or Study. Switch with
  ⌃1–9, ⌘⌥ arrows, or by swiping two fingers sideways on the sidebar.
- **One profile per space, or shared.** Each profile keeps its own cookies,
  history, passwords, extensions, search engine and downloads, and tabs from
  different profiles still sit in one window.
- **Themes** per space: pick a colour and how see-through the window is. Give
  each space a name and an icon.
- **Link rules** send sites to a chosen space, so work links always open in
  Work.

## Command bar

- **⌘T or ⌘L** opens a command bar built on Chrome's omnibox, with history,
  inline completion and your search engine.
- **Switch to an open tab** instead of opening it twice.
- **Faster loads**: Argon starts connecting to the highlighted result before you
  press Return.

## Little Argon

- **⌥⌘N in any app** opens a floating search bar, and the result opens in a
  small Little Argon window.
- **Links from other apps** can open in Little Argon instead of your main
  window. Move the page into a space when you want to keep it.
- **⌥⌘-click** a link to open it in Little Argon.

## Pages

- **Find in page**, zoom, print and save.
- **Capture Page** takes a screenshot of the page.
- **Copy URL** (⇧⌘C) and **Copy URL as Markdown**.
- **Automatic Picture in Picture**: a playing video pops out when you switch
  tabs and goes back when you return.
- **Docked DevTools**, JavaScript console and view source.
- **Site panel** from the address pill: share, Capture Page, find on page,
  DevTools, and the site's settings and connection security.
- **Mouse back and forward buttons**, including Logitech mice that send them
  as swipes.

## Chrome compatibility

- **Extensions** from the Chrome Web Store.
- **uBlock Origin Lite** comes installed. Remove it from Extensions if you
  don't want it; it won't come back.
- **Passwords and autofill**, with offers to save and update logins.
- **Passkeys** through your phone or a security key.
- **Permissions**: camera, microphone, location and notification requests
  appear as a card under the address pill. If macOS has turned one off for
  Argon, the card links to the right pane in System Settings.
- **HTTP auth, file uploads and downloads**, all using Chrome's own logic.

## Import

- **Bring your stuff over** from Arc or Google Chrome: spaces, pinned tabs,
  folders and favorites from Arc, or bookmarks from each Chrome profile, plus
  history, saved passwords and cookies.

## Memory and speed

- **Sleeping tabs**: tabs you haven't looked at for a while free their memory
  and reload when you open them. Tabs playing media, using the camera or
  screen, or holding form input stay awake.
- **Memory pressure**: when macOS runs low on memory, the oldest background
  tabs go to sleep early.
- **Low Power Mode**: background tabs pause after 5 minutes.
- **Profiles load on demand** and unload after 30 minutes of not being used.

## Privacy

- **No AI features**: Chromium's built-in AI, its AI web APIs and background
  model downloads are turned off.
- **No field trials**: Chromium's built-in experiment configuration is off.
- **Clear browsing data** for the current profile.

## App

- **Frameless window** option where the page runs to the window's edges.
- **Shortcuts list** in Settings → Shortcuts, searchable by action or key.
- **App icon** choices: Argon, Daybreak, Dusk, Sunset and Tide.
- **In-place updates** from the card at the bottom of the sidebar, checked
  against GitHub releases every few hours.
- **Warn before quitting** with ⌘Q pressed twice.
